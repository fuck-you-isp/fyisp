How to read these charts
  • Lines break where no probe succeeded. The strip under each chart shows lost samples by reason; grey hatching means not measured (fyisp was not running), which is not loss.
  • Drag on a chart to zoom, double-click to reset. Click a legend entry to isolate it, Ctrl/Cmd-click to toggle.
  • Faint coloured bands on the charts mark incidents from the outage log; click an incident to zoom to it.
  • Choose “show speed tests” in a chart’s legend to mark when speed tests ran (zoom in to see each one as a band). Samples taken during a test are measured as usual, so a spike there may be the test.
  • ×2.3 normal: 2.3 times slower than usual for this target (its typical time over the past week). The “Normal band” shades each target’s usual range (median to p95).
Glossary
MOS
Call quality score from 1 (unusable) to 4.5 (excellent), estimated from delay, dropouts and jitter. Good ≥ 4.0, Fair ≥ 3.6, Poor ≥ 3.1.
Jitter
How unevenly packets arrive. Steady below 10 ms; noticeable from 30 ms, when call apps add delay to smooth it out.
Loss (packet loss)
Share of test messages that never came back. Above about 5%, people on a call start to hear gaps. “Not measured” is never counted as loss.
RTT (round-trip time)
Time for a message to reach a server and come back, in milliseconds. Under 150 ms feels instant on a call.
STUN
A tiny “what’s my address?” request that call apps send before a call. fyisp uses it to test the path call audio takes.
Burst
5 STUN requests sent quickly, 20 ms apart, like call audio packets. Loss and jitter are measured per burst.
UDP
The fast, no-retry way call audio and video travel. If it’s blocked, calls fall back to slower routes.
TCP
The reliable way web pages and apps connect. fyisp times how long opening a connection takes.
ICMP (ping)
The “are you there?” message used to check your router and provider. Some equipment answers it slowly on purpose.
Gateway
Your router: the first box every message from this device passes through.
ISP edge
The first piece of your internet provider’s equipment past your router.
Anycast
Big services (Cloudflare and Google DNS) that answer from a site near almost every provider. If they’re fine, the wider internet is fine.
p95
95 out of 100 measurements were faster than this. Shows the slow moments the median hides.
Median
The middle value: half the measurements were faster, half slower.
DNS
The internet’s address book: it turns names like zoom.us into addresses. If it fails, apps can’t be found even though the connection works.
