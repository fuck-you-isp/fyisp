Lines break where no probe succeeded. The strip under each chart shows lost samples by reason; grey hatching means not measured (fyisp was not running), which is not loss. Drag on a chart to zoom, double-click to reset. Click a legend entry to isolate it, Ctrl/Cmd-click to toggle. Faint coloured bands on the charts mark incidents from the outage log; click an incident to zoom to it. Flags mark notes. On your own dashboard, right-click a chart (long-press on a phone) to add a note at that time, or Shift-drag to add one over a range; click a flag to edit it. Blue flags are shown on your public link. "×2.3 normal" means a target is 2.3 times slower than its usual latency (its median over the past week). The "Normal band" shades each target's usual range (median to p95).
